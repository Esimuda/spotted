# Demo video shot list

Under two minutes. Film the phone, not a desktop, if you can. The last shot should be the real animal, not the screen.

Replace the bracketed lines when you record. Do not invent the outdoor bit.

| Time | Picture | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:08 | Phone in your hand, outdoors if you are. Open the landing page. | "I keep a lot of my week in calls and group chats. This is Spotted. See nature. Share it. Then the screen is supposed to end." |
| 0:08–0:18 | Tap **Open the field**. Show **This phone** and **Server**. | "The name comes from CLIP, an open model. On a signal I can use the server. With no signal, the same model runs on the phone after one download." |
| 0:18–0:32 | Tap a reference photo, or take a real one. Hold on the viewfinder until the name appears. | "One photo. It only knows the species in this guide, and it says so when it is unsure." |
| 0:32–0:48 | Result: common name, scientific name, habitat, diet, behaviour, status. Mention the soft pin if the species is sensitive. | "The facts are in the app. Gemma can rewrite them, and if it wanders we keep the guide. Rare species never get a precise pin." |
| 0:48–1:02 | Save. Open the trail. Tap wonder or protect. Open the kingfisher note and the comments. | "Then it is a small trail. People can react. The demo photos are references, so the feed is not empty before you add your own." |
| 1:02–1:16 | Map. Point at a green dot, then a clay circle around Okomu or Yankari. | "Green is a place someone chose to share. Clay is about eleven kilometres, on purpose." |
| 1:16–1:28 | Life list. Streak, species, the grid. | "A life list and a streak. The point is to want the next walk, not the next scroll." |
| 1:28–1:45 | Put the phone down, or turn it over. If you are outside, let the real thing fill the frame. | "[YOUR LINE AFTER YOU ACTUALLY USE IT OUTSIDE. One sentence: what you saw, and whether you put the phone away.]" |

Total about 1:45.

If the phone model is not cached yet, switch to **Server** before the photo so the name arrives in a few seconds. Say that the weights are the same model.
